Round 1 plan. I want to measure three basic kinds of cue before building anything:

- **R1F1, colour layout (32 neurons):** a 4×4 grid of brightness cells and a 4×4 grid of blue-minus-red cells. These are linear, with no ReLU. I expect sky to make the top cells bluish for parachute and church, warm colours to push cells negative for tench, French horn and chain saw, and white backgrounds to make cells bright for cassette player and golf ball.
- **R1F2, edges and texture (32 neurons):** the image is shrunk to 32×32, then 8 edge filters each give an average edge strength in the 4 quadrants. The filters are: vertical edges, horizontal edges, two diagonals, a texture filter, red-green colour edges, blue-yellow horizontal edges and a checkerboard (very fine detail). I expect parachute photos to be smooth (little texture), gas pumps and cassette players to have many straight vertical and horizontal edges, grass and fur (springer) to be highly textured, and church photos to have strong horizontal edges near the horizon.
- **R1F3, colour fractions (40 neurons):** 10 colour detectors (sky blue, green, white, dark, red, yellow/orange, warm, deep blue, teal, purple). Each one reports what fraction of each 32×32 quadrant has that colour. I expect: sky blue and deep blue in the top quadrants for parachute and church, green in the bottom for golf ball, springer and church, red for gas pump and garbage truck, yellow for tench, horn and golf ball, white for cassette player, chain saw and golf ball, and dark for French horn and chain saw.
