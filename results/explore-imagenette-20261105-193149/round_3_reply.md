Round 3 plan. Where things stand:

- **Strong cues so far:** parachute is smooth and bluish. Cassette player and chain saw have white backgrounds and many horizontal edges. Golf ball has green. French horn has fine texture and is dark. Gas pump and springer have many vertical edges. Church has sky at the top.
- **Still hard to separate:** tench, springer, church, French horn, garbage truck and gas pump. The edge neurons mostly tracked overall edge strength and a weak-versus-strong pair carries almost the same information. The colour-presence neurons tracked each other closely, with brass, yellow and skin at r ≈ 0.94–0.98.

This round adds new information rather than more of the same:

- **R3F1, coarse structure (40 neurons):** the image is shrunk to 16×16, so these edges are large-scale lines (truck outlines, church walls, pump bodies) rather than fine texture. There are 10 filters: 5 brightness-edge filters plus colour edges (red-green, blue-yellow, green-excess). Each filter reports top-left, top-right, bottom-left and bottom-right separately, so the final layer can see whether structure sits at the top (church) or bottom (truck, grass).
- **R3F2, colour layout (32 neurons):** a 4×4 grid of green excess. Together with R1F1's brightness and blue-minus-red grids, this makes the full 4×4 colour thumbnail available to the final layer. It adds a 4×4 grid of near-white fraction, which shows where the white background or ball is.
- **R3F3, full-resolution texture (12 neurons):** whole-image edge strength at 64×64 for brightness edges in four directions, spots, and fine checker texture (fur, grass, engraved brass). It also includes colour-opponent edges (red-green, blue-yellow, green, red-blue), which should catch red pumps and trucks against their surroundings.
